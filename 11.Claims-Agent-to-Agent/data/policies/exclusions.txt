Section 9.3 - General Exclusions
The following are not covered under any section of this policy: ordinary wear
and tear; mechanical or electrical breakdown not arising from a covered peril;
damage from deferred maintenance or gradual deterioration; loss arising while
the vehicle or property is used for commercial hire unless endorsed; damage
intentionally caused by the insured; and any loss where material facts have been
misrepresented. Misrepresentation voids the claim and may void the policy.
