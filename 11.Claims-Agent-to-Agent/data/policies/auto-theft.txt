Section 4.4 - Theft and Total Loss (Auto)
Theft of the insured vehicle is covered where the loss is reported to police
within 48 hours and a police reference number is provided. Where the vehicle is
not recovered within 30 days it is treated as a total loss and settled at the
replacement value determined by an approved valuation, less the comprehensive
deductible. Claims for vehicles left unsecured, with keys in the vehicle, or
left in a long-stay facility beyond the declared usage terms may be reduced or
declined. Total loss settlements above 20,000 USD require senior adjuster review.
