Section 2.5 - Deductibles and Settlement Arithmetic
Every covered loss is settled net of the deductible stated on the policy
schedule for that peril. The deductible is subtracted from the approved loss
amount, not from the amount requested by the claimant. Where a claimant requests
an amount exceeding the approved estimate, settlement is capped at the approved
estimate. Deductibles are applied once per incident, not per damaged item.
