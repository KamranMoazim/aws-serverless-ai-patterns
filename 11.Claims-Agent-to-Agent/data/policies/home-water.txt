Section 7.2 - Sudden Water Discharge (Home)
Sudden and accidental discharge of water from a plumbing supply line, appliance
or fixture is covered, including resulting damage to flooring, cabinetry and
fixed structures. The policyholder must take reasonable steps to mitigate the
loss promptly; documented same-day mitigation supports the claim. Gradual
seepage, long-term leaks and damage arising from deferred maintenance are
excluded under Section 9.3. The water damage deductible applies per incident.
