Section 12.1 - Payout Authority and Human Approval
Settlements below 5,000 USD may be released automatically once coverage is
confirmed and the fraud risk level is LOW or MEDIUM. Settlements of 5,000 USD or
more require documented approval by an authorized human adjuster before funds are
released. Any claim carrying a HIGH fraud risk level must not be paid regardless
of amount, and must be routed to the investigations team. Automated systems may
recommend a settlement but must not release funds above the automatic threshold.
