Section 4.1 - Collision Coverage (Auto)
Northwind Mutual covers direct and accidental damage to the insured vehicle
arising from collision with another vehicle or a fixed object, including
guardrails, barriers and road furniture. Coverage applies whether or not the
insured is at fault. Payment is the lesser of the repair estimate from an
approved shop or the actual cash value of the vehicle, less the collision
deductible stated on the policy schedule. Single-vehicle collisions caused by
road conditions, including wet or icy surfaces, are covered under this section.
